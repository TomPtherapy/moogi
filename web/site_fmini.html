    Acid Moogi: a personal fork of FMini-1 (sequencer on the keys, an acid line generator ported from a norns mod).
    
    FMini-1 by Ruppert, a custom firmware based on Felucca by Leo Kuroshita (@kurogedelic), Hügelton Instruments, with its CrispyZebra waveforms, drum voices, Sample Pack and Fukiai icons (MIT).
    
    Font: Inter Tight (The Inter Project Authors, SIL OFL 1.1). Samples: Versilian Studios VSCO-2 Community Edition and VCSL (CC0 1.0).
    
    FM6 engine: msfa from Dexed (Google Inc., Pascal Gauthier, Apache-2.0). The package also holds three JieLi AC79 SDK files, uboot.boot, cfg_tool.bin and eq_cfg_hw.bin (JieLi Technology, Apache-2.0). Licences: LICENSING, GPL-3.0, all.
